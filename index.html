SparshaGuard

A safety smartwatch where a stranger's touch raises the alarm. Once Guard Mode is on, another person's grab starts a 15-second PIN countdown; no PIN sends her location to her contacts and calls 112.

Digital twin

The watch's own firmware running on a simulated sensor, in real time.
