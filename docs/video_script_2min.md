# SparshaGuard: 2-minute video script

Built around the demo's **Play all** run (80 s, timings measured in the browser), with a short
intro before it and the science after it. Narration is about 150 words a minute; the on-screen
captions already say the key facts, so the voice can be calm and brief.

## Setup before recording

1. Open `demo/sparshaguard_demo.html` in Chrome, press F11 for full screen, sound on.
2. Have a title slide ready (SparshaGuard logo, team names, RV University).
3. Start the screen recorder (Windows: Win + Alt + R or OBS; Mac: Cmd + Shift + 5).

## Script

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Title slide | "Most safety apps need her to find her phone, unlock it and press SOS. In an attack, there's no time for that." |
| 0:08–0:15 | Demo page, idle. Click **Play all** at 0:15 | "SparshaGuard flips the default. Once it's on, someone else's touch raises the alarm by itself." |
| 0:15–0:25 | Ch 1: Guard Mode, watch learns, then "Guarding" | "Ananya turns on Guard Mode. The watch learns her own body for five seconds, then keeps checking the skin around it, five times a second." |
| 0:25–0:42 | Ch 2: stranger grabs her wrist; ring fills; PIN countdown; alert; Amma's phone | "A stranger grabs her wrist. The signal jumps from a tenth of a percent to sixteen. Held for two seconds, the watch vibrates and asks for her PIN. She can't answer, so her location goes to her contacts, 112 is called, and audio is recorded." |
| 0:42–0:51 | Re-arm, then Ch 3: friend holds her hand, PIN 1234, Cancelled | "A friend's hand looks the same to the sensor, so the PIN decides. She types it: cancelled." |
| 0:51–1:06 | Ch 4: forced to cancel, PIN 9999, "Cancelled", silent alert on the phone | "If she's forced to cancel, she types her duress PIN. The watch shows 'Cancelled', but her mother still gets a silent alert." |
| 1:06–1:15 | Ch 5: the watch is ripped off, alert in under a second | "Rip the watch off, and it alerts at once: the electrode loses her skin." |
| 1:15–1:35 | Ch 6: everyday life, brief touch ignored, bystander, shoulder grab | "Brief touches and people standing close don't set it off. And honestly: a wrist sensor can't feel a grab on her shoulder. That's what our planned motion and audio sensing is for." |
| 1:35–1:52 | Click **Show the science**; point at the four numbers and the sweep | "Every number comes from fifteen published papers. We checked the circuit model against SPICE, and the 3D field against MIT's FastCap. In random trials, it catches most wrist grabs and every strap removal, with about four unwanted prompts an hour." |
| 1:52–2:00 | Title slide again | "Next: our battery-powered prototype, to measure what's left. SparshaGuard: the touch itself is the alarm." |

## Tips

- If a chapter runs a little long, trim the pause after the alert in editing rather than rushing
  the voice.
- Record the demo once without narration, then record the voice over it; it is easier to time.
- Keep "honestly" in the shoulder line: judges trust a team that shows its limits.
