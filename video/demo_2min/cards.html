SparshaGuard

A safety smartwatch where a stranger's touch raises the alarm. A demonstration on its digital twin.

Co-developers: Bhajanth V · Sukruth A Chinnur

The problem · India, 2022
4,45,256
crimes against women registered: about 51 every hour
18.7 %
were assaults to outrage a woman's modesty: a grab, an unwanted touch
In that moment there is no time to unlock a phone and press SOS.
SparshaGuard flips it: the touch itself raises the alarm.
Source: NCRB, Crime in India 2022
What the digital twin shows
70 %of grabs caught over 7 body places (a touch sensor alone: 13 %)
99 %of struggles caught, even with no touch felt
0–1unwanted PIN prompts in 9 simulated hours (touch sensor alone: 24–28)
100 %of strap removals caught

Simulated, not yet measured: physics from 60 published sources, checked with SPICE and MIT FastCap. Next: a battery-powered prototype (Gate 1) to measure it.
