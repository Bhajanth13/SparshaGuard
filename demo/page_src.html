Firmware log

    Same model as the study: a wrist grab changes the reading by ~16 %, every other grab (forearm included) by about the noise (~0.1 %). In 120 random trials each, this twin catches ~87 % of wrist grabs (the rest: his palm misses the outer electrode), 0 % of forearm and shoulder grabs, and 100 % of strap removals; ~4 unwanted PIN prompts per hour of daily life (study: 93 %, 0 %, 0 %, ~5.5 per hour). Grabs away from the wrist need the planned motion and audio paths.
    Sensor values are simulated from the published-source model (sg_model.py), not measured; Gate 1 hardware will calibrate them. Location and contacts are made up.
