Each reading is a sweep of 200 frequencies (1 kHz–3.5 MHz), 5 times a second. The static channel reads her body voltage 500 times a second: a touch by another person shares charge in under a millisecond, while her footsteps and movements are slower. Static levels on Indian floors are not measured yet, so the numbers above use ~10 V. The physics uses values from published papers; the circuit solver is checked against SPICE and the 3D field against MIT's FastCap. The sensor here is simulated; the decisions are the watch firmware's own, and it never sees which scene is playing. Gate 1 hardware will calibrate the numbers.
