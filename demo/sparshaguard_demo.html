Each reading is a sweep of 200 frequencies (1 kHz–3.5 MHz), 5 times a second. The physics uses values from 15 published papers; the circuit solver is checked against SPICE and the 3D field against MIT's FastCap. The sensor here is simulated; the decisions are the watch firmware's own, and it never sees which scene is playing. Gate 1 hardware will calibrate the numbers.
